SpaceGraphJS Physics Demo

This demo showcases the physics system:

  • Verlet Physics - Particle-based physics simulation
  • Spring Constraints - Connecting surfaces with springs
  • Collision Detection - Preventing surface overlaps
  • Force-Directed Layout - Automatic arrangement of connected surfaces
  • Physics Containers - Specialized containers for physics simulations
